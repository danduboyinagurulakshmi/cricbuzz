==================================================
IMPORTANT CONSTRAINTS
==================================================

1. Each player must have a unique player_id.

2. Each team must have a unique team_id.

3. Each venue must have a unique venue_id.

4. Each series must have a unique series_id.

5. Each match must have a unique match_id.

6. A team should not appear more than once in the same match.

7. A match should normally contain two participating teams.

8. team_position identifies the team's position in the match.

9. batting_position identifies a player's position in the batting order.

10. batsman1_position and batsman2_position are used to
    identify consecutive batting positions.

11. batsman1_id and batsman2_id must refer to valid players.

12. victory_type should represent the type of victory,
    such as runs or wickets.

13. victory_margin stores the numerical winning margin.

14. Toss decision should represent the decision made by
    the toss-winning team, such as bat or bowl.


==================================================
NORMALIZATION
==================================================

First Normal Form (1NF)
- All columns contain atomic values.
- No lists are stored inside individual cells.
- Repeating groups are avoided.

Second Normal Form (2NF)
- Tables are in 1NF.
- Non-key attributes depend on the complete primary key.
- Surrogate primary keys are used for major entities.

Third Normal Form (3NF)
- Tables are in 2NF.
- Non-key attributes do not depend on other non-key attributes.
- Player, team, venue, series, match, and performance
  information is separated into appropriate tables.
